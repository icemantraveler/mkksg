Master Koin Code:
1. Choose a VS. game and load a profile for each fighter.
Make sure both profiles have plenty of coins.
2. Place a normal one-match wager and complete the first fight.
3. Afterward, have *Player 1 only* open the wager tag.
Player 2 must leave theirs closed.
4. Player 1 presses / to trigger the coin sound, then to place the wager.
Once the sound stops, press the wager button again to close the tag.
5. Select characters and fight again, with Player 1 defeating Player 2.

Result: Player 1 earns coins, but Player 2 loses nothing!
Repeat this exploit to earn enough coins to unlock every Krypt coffin in under an hour.

Hit to open the wager window.

When Player 1 opens the wager tab, use the following coin values to determine your bet, as the selected coin type isn't displayed.
x1 = Onyx
x2 = Jade
x3 = Ruby
x4 = Gold
x5 = Platinum
x6 = Sapphire
Arena Select:
At the fighter selection screen, press to open the stage selection menu.
Use / on the D-Pad to choose a stage, then press to confirm.
You'll return to fighter selection to choose your fighter.

Note: Only unlocked stages are available.
